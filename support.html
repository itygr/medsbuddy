MedsBuddy Support

Need help or found a problem? Email itygrmedia@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

MedsBuddy is a reminder helper, not medical advice. For medical questions call your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency call 911. In the US, Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222.

Common questions

I'm not getting reminders.

Open iPhone Settings > Notifications > MedsBuddy and make sure notifications are allowed. Silent mode, Focus modes and low battery can delay alerts, so never rely on any app alone.

How do I scan a label?

Tap Scan on the Today tab (a MedsBuddy Plus feature). Review and confirm every detail before saving. Scans can be wrong.

How do I share with another adult?

Settings > Family sharing > Invite (a MedsBuddy Plus feature). It uses your own iCloud and needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud.

How do I cancel MedsBuddy Plus?

iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > MedsBuddy, or use "Manage subscription" in the app's Settings. Reminders for people you already added keep working.

Where is my data?

Only on your device, see the Privacy Policy. Deleting the app deletes it, so use Export to PDF first if you want a copy.
