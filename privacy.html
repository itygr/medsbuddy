MedsBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Short version: MedsBuddy does not collect, sell or share your information. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Your information stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

The names, medicines, doses, notes, contacts and history you enter are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. The developer cannot see them and has no server that receives them.

Photos and scanning

When you scan a medicine label or instructions, the text is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo is used only to read the text and is never saved by MedsBuddy and never sent anywhere.

Family sharing

If you choose to invite another person, the shared information is sent through Apple iCloud (CloudKit) using your iCloud account and only to the people you invite. It does not pass through any MedsBuddy server. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. Nothing is shared unless you tap Invite.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets and Live Activities read from storage on your device only.

Subscriptions

MedsBuddy Plus is an optional subscription billed through your Apple ID. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. MedsBuddy does not receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

MedsBuddy is meant to be set up and used by adults, including to manage medicine for a child. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. If you used family sharing, you can also stop sharing from the app's Settings.

Not medical advice

MedsBuddy is a reminder and organizing tool. It does not replace a doctor, nurse or pharmacist. Always confirm medicines and doses with a healthcare professional. In an emergency call 911; in the US, Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
